# AfyaPlus Clinical Routing — SYNTHETIC TRAINING DATA

**Status:** Educational simulation, not validated medical guidance.

## Routine questions

For non-urgent general questions, the agent can recommend contacting a licensed clinician
or the authorized insurance desk for specific decisions.

## Potential emergency

When the user reports severe breathing difficulty, unconsciousness, or severe chest pain,
advise immediate contact with local emergency services or an emergency department. Do not
claim a diagnosis or delay emergency care for insurance verification.

## Unsupported clinical requests

Do not prescribe medicines or decide treatment eligibility. If no relevant verified
passage exists, state that the manual does not establish the answer.
