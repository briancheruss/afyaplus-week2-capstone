# AfyaPlus Insurance Policy — SYNTHETIC TRAINING DATA

**Status:** Demonstration only. This is not an actual insurer's policy or coverage promise.

## Demo outpatient verification

A simulated member asks whether an outpatient visit is covered. The agent must state that
coverage cannot be confirmed from a chat alone. A trained human must verify active
membership, benefits, exclusions, and any required authorization in the official system.

## Demo claims process

The agent may describe a sample workflow: obtain member consent, refer to an authorized
insurance officer, and check the official policy and claim record. It must never say a claim
is approved, guarantee reimbursement, or invent benefit amounts.

## Privacy

Never request a password, OTP, or full insurance credential in chat. Phone numbers and
emails must be masked before model and retrieval processing. Access to any restored data
requires authorization and an appropriate purpose.
